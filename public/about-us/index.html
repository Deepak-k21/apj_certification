Our story

Stronger systems.
More capable people.

APJ Certification was established by D. Prabhu to support organisations through auditing and internal and external training.

APJ CERTIFICATION is a Auditing, Internal and External Training company based in Singapore established by D.Prabhu With more than 15 years of experience, we are dedicated to provide Workplace Safety & Health, Environmental and Audit services to various industries.

APJ’s  Auditors are highly qualified as WSH professionals; each having Bachelor degree in the field of engineering, safety and health; specialist diplomas in occupational safety and health through recognized educational institutions.
