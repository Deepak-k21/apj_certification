Supervision Challenges in Foundation Works

About this programme

Turn understanding
into practical capability.

Explore construction and inspection challenges in piling, excavation and other foundation activities.

Discuss your current experience, learning objectives and any prerequisites with APJ so the programme can be matched to your requirements.

What you’ll explore

  • Recognise ground-condition, excavation, piling and groundwater risks
  • Identify key inspection records, test results and deviations that need review
  • Coordinate reporting and escalation when site conditions differ from design assumptions

Who should attend?

REs, RTOs and construction supervisors involved in foundation works.

This is a proposed APJ RE/RTO training topic. STU recognition has not been confirmed. APJ will confirm approval status, certificate arrangements and the final syllabus before enrolment.
