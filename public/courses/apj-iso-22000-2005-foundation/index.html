About this programme

Turn understanding
into practical capability.

Understand the principles, terminology and requirements of ISO 22000:2005 and build a confident foundation for your next step.

Discuss your current experience, learning objectives and any prerequisites with APJ so the programme can be matched to your requirements.

What you’ll explore

  • Understand the terminology, structure and principles of the management system
  • Recognise how the requirements apply to your organisation
  • Identify appropriate next steps for further implementation or auditing development

Who should attend?

Management system professionals, process owners, supervisors and people developing implementation or auditing skills.
