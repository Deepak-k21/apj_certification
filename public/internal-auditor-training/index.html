Before you register

A little clarity goes a long way.

Which training pathway should I choose?

Foundation courses introduce a management standard. Implementation and auditing pathways build on that knowledge. Discuss the appropriate level and any prerequisites with APJ.

Can I register my whole team?

Yes. Choose company sponsorship and add the participants for each selected course. You can review their details before submitting your request.

When do I pay?

Registration begins as a request. APJ confirms the course date, fee, delivery format and certificate arrangements before payment. Secure Razorpay checkout becomes available for configured offers after merchant activation.

Are RE/RTO renewal approvals confirmed?

The 11 listed topics are proposed APJ training programmes. STU recognition, duration, dates, fees and certificate arrangements must be confirmed by APJ before enrolment.
